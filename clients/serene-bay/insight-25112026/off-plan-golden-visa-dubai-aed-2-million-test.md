An off-plan golden visa in Dubai is decided mainly by the price on the contract, not by how much of that price has been paid. The Dubai Land Department's own service page describes the 10-year residence permit as open to an investor owning property "the purchase value of which is equal to or more than 2 million AED at the time of purchase", and says the property may be mortgaged. Since January 2024, when [The National reported](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2024/01/24/golden-visa-dubai-property/) that the old minimum down payment of AED 1 million (or 50 per cent of the value) had been cancelled, a buyer on a developer payment plan has not needed to fund half the unit before applying.

Three things still decide whether a particular purchase works. The price must clear AED 2 million at the time of purchase, not later. The DLD's terms say the property must be "wholly owned by the investor", which matters to couples buying together. And the same DLD page still carries two different descriptions of what a mortgaged owner must prove. A buyer who treats the threshold as a formality can end up with a unit that is AED 20,000 short, or a joint title that does not count.

## How the off-plan golden visa rule got here

The property route to residency has been rewritten several times, and much of what circulates online describes an earlier version. The dates below are the dates each change was reported, with the source that reported it.

| When reported | What changed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| September 2021 | Minimum property value for the shorter investor visa cut from AED 1 million to AED 750,000 | [Khaleej Times, citing the DLD website](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/minimum-investment-for-3-year-visa-cut-to-dh750000-in-dubai) |
| January 2024 | AED 1 million (or 50 per cent) minimum down payment for the 10-year visa removed; AED 2 million value threshold kept | [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2024/01/24/golden-visa-dubai-property/), [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/visa-and-immigration-in-uae/uae-golden-visa-dubai-drops-minimum-down-payment-required-for-property-owners) |
| April 2026 | 2-year investor visa: no minimum value for a sole owner; AED 400,000 minimum share for each joint owner | [Gulf News, citing the DLD's Cube centre](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-scraps-minimum-property-value-for-solo-investor-visas-sets-joint-ownership-floor-what-it-means-1.500523065) |
| Current DLD terms | 10-year visa: purchase value of AED 2 million or more, wholly owned, mortgage permitted with a bank letter | [DLD service page](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-golden-visa-investor/) |

Two cautions sit under that table. The January 2024 change arrived without a published circular: The National's report rested on a law firm's account and a broker's visit to The Cube, and the DLD told the paper at the time that the criteria "stays unchanged". Practice moved anyway, and later coverage treats the change as settled. The April 2026 change to the 2-year visa was also, [according to Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/visa-immigration/dubai-residency-by-investment-guide-1.500523250), not formally announced; it appeared on The Cube's guidance. Neither was set out in a law a buyer can read. That is the reason to confirm the current position at the point of booking rather than relying on an article, this one included.

## Purchase value, not paid amount

The DLD test looks at the price "at the time of purchase". Read plainly, that has two consequences for an off-plan buyer.

First, the qualifying moment is the sale, not the valuation. A unit bought at AED 1.98 million does not become eligible because the tower is worth more at handover. Second, the amount paid so far is not the test for an unmortgaged buyer on a developer plan. The [Khaleej Times report of January 2024](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/visa-and-immigration-in-uae/uae-golden-visa-dubai-drops-minimum-down-payment-required-for-property-owners) quotes a sales progression head at Allsopp & Allsopp: "There is no minimum equity investment."

The worked example below uses an AED 2.1 million unit on a typical construction-linked plan. The instalment amounts are our calculation; the eligibility column reflects the DLD terms as written.

| Stage | Share of price paid | Amount paid (our calculation) | Purchase value | Clears the AED 2 million value test? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking | 10 per cent | AED 210,000 | AED 2,100,000 | Yes, on value |
| Mid-construction | 50 per cent | AED 1,050,000 | AED 2,100,000 | Yes, on value |
| Handover, 50 per cent mortgaged | 50 per cent cash, 50 per cent bank | AED 1,050,000 own funds | AED 2,100,000 | Yes on value; see the mortgage wording below |

The price that counts is the one registered with the DLD. A discount negotiated after booking, or a developer "cashback" that reduces the registered price, can take a unit below the line. A fee waiver that leaves the registered price untouched should not. Check the figure on the registration, not the brochure.

## What the DLD page says about a mortgage

Here the official wording does not agree with itself. The page's description says that for a mortgaged property "a bank letter indicating 2 million AED paid amount as a proof to be provided". Its terms, a few lines further down, ask for a no-objection letter from the bank "indicating the paid amount and the balance", with no minimum stated.

The first reading would require AED 2 million of the buyer's own money to be paid, which is close to the pre-2024 rule in reverse. The second only asks the bank to disclose the position and confirm it does not object. Press reports since 2024, including [Gulf News in April 2026](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-scraps-minimum-property-value-for-solo-investor-visas-sets-joint-ownership-floor-what-it-means-1.500523065), describe mortgaged assets as eligible on value. A buyer planning to mortgage at handover should ask the lender, before signing, whether it issues the no-objection letter for residency and what the letter will state. Without that letter, the application cannot meet the DLD's terms.

## Where off-plan sits in the documents

The DLD's list of required documents names an "e-Certificate of Title or title deed". An off-plan buyer does not yet hold either; what exists is the registration on the [preliminary register (Oqood)](/insights/oqood-registration-dubai-off-plan-what-it-proves). Coverage since 2024, from [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2024/01/24/golden-visa-dubai-property/) to [Gulf News in 2026](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/visa-immigration/dubai-residency-by-investment-guide-1.500523250), states that off-plan property qualifies, and in practice it is the Oqood registration that evidences ownership before handover. A unit that has only a reservation form and a receipt is not registered, and so has nothing for the application to rest on.

Two practical checks follow. The developer must have registered the sale, which normally happens once the booking instalment and registration fee are paid. And the developer and project must be registered with the regulator, since an unregistered sale leaves no record at the DLD.

## Joint ownership and the visa tiers

The 10-year terms say the AED 2 million must be "wholly owned by the investor (one or more properties) under the name of the applicant". The page does not deal with joint ownership. A couple buying an AED 2.4 million unit in equal shares holds AED 1.2 million each on paper, and the cautious reading is that neither share clears the line alone. The practical route is usually for one spouse to hold title and sponsor the other, but the choice has inheritance and financing consequences, so it is worth settling before the name goes on the contract.

The tiers below are the ones property can support in Dubai, as currently described.

| Visa | Property condition | Other conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2-year investor | No minimum for a sole owner; AED 400,000 minimum share per joint owner | Registered ownership | [Gulf News, April 2026](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-scraps-minimum-property-value-for-solo-investor-visas-sets-joint-ownership-floor-what-it-means-1.500523065) |
| 5-year retirement | AED 1 million in property | Aged 55 or over; alternative financial criteria apply | [Gulf News, April 2026](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/visa-immigration/dubai-residency-by-investment-guide-1.500523250) |
| 10-year golden | Purchase value of AED 2 million or more, wholly owned, one or more properties | Applicant inside the UAE; bank letter if mortgaged | [DLD service page](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-golden-visa-investor/) |

The reports on the 2-year visa do not say whether off-plan units qualify for it, so a buyer relying on that tier should ask.

## Cost and timing of the application

The DLD lists the investor's own 10-year permit at a total of AED 9,884.75, made up of a medical examination (AED 700), Emirates ID (AED 1,153), residency confirmation (AED 2,856.75), DLD fees (AED 4,020) and administrative fees (AED 1,155). Each family member's permit is a further AED 5,774.50, with AED 318.75 to open the family sponsorship file. The stated completion time is seven to ten business days, and the investor must attend in person from inside the country. These figures come from the [DLD service page](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-golden-visa-investor/) and are subject to change.

## What the threshold does to unit choice

A fixed line at AED 2 million bends the market around it. As early as March 2024, [Khaleej Times reported](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-golden-visa-demand-prompts-investors-to-raise-budget-developers-to-increase-unit-sizes) a brokerage chief executive describing buyers with budgets of AED 1.5 million and more raising them to AED 2 million, and developers enlarging units to meet them. That is one firm's account, not market data, but the mechanism is easy to see. A unit priced at AED 2.05 million because it clears the line may be worth less, per square foot, than one at AED 1.9 million in the same tower.

The sensible order is to choose the unit on its merits first, then check whether it clears the threshold, and only then decide whether the residency is worth paying for the gap. Two units that both clear the line can be compared directly on price per square foot, payment plan and handover record. Paying a premium for the visa is a legitimate choice. Paying it without noticing is not.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get an off-plan golden visa in Dubai before handover?

Yes, on the DLD's current terms, if the registered purchase price is AED 2 million or more and the sale is registered on the preliminary register (Oqood). Reports since January 2024 state there is no minimum down payment. Confirm current practice with the [DLD](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-golden-visa-investor/) at booking, as several changes have been made through guidance rather than published law.

### Does the AED 2 million include the DLD registration fee?

The DLD's terms refer to the property's purchase value at the time of purchase, which is the price registered for the unit. The [DLD registration fee](/insights/dld-fee-waiver-off-plan-what-it-is-worth) and other costs are paid on top of that price and are not described as part of it. A buyer whose price sits just under AED 2 million should not assume fees close the gap.

### Can two people combine shares to reach AED 2 million?

The DLD's terms say the property must be wholly owned by the investor, and the page does not address joint ownership. On that wording, two AED 1.2 million shares in one unit do not each clear the line. Where both partners need residency, the usual route is for one to qualify and sponsor the other as a spouse.

### What happens to the visa if I sell the unit?

The visa rests on owning qualifying property. Selling or assigning the unit removes that basis, and a buyer who plans to [sell before handover](/insights/selling-off-plan-before-handover) should expect the residency to be affected. Holding several properties that together still total AED 2 million keeps the condition met, according to the DLD's wording on one or more properties.

### Will my bank provide the letter if I take a mortgage at handover?

Do not assume it will. The DLD requires a no-objection letter from the bank stating the paid amount and the balance, and one line on its page refers to AED 2 million paid. Ask the lender in writing before you sign what its letter will state, and whether it issues one for residency purposes at all.
